A single wedding vow spoken by a robot at its own wedding. The line is built
entirely from machine vocabulary — a boot sequence, a reboot, a battery
running down — but the promise underneath is an ordinary married one: you
come first, I will be patient even when we argue, and whatever I have left
at the end is yours. The laugh comes from the situation itself (patience as
something that occasionally needs rebooting), and the tenderness is real
enough to survive a ceremony.

## The entry

> I vow to keep you first in my boot sequence, to reboot my patience whenever we argue, and to spend my last percent of battery holding your hand.

144 characters, one sentence, one line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
